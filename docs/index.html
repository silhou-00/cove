What is Cove

One person, one phone, nothing extra running.

Cove is a single-user, offline-first task manager. Everything you type in, tasks, schedules, recurring items, categories, is stored only in a local database on your phone. There's no account, no login, and no backend server involved at any point.

Type a task in plain language and Quick Add figures out the category, priority, and schedule for you. From there Cove gives you a few different ways to look at the same list: what's happening today, what needs attention soon, and a weekly view of anything you've time-blocked.
