01b — Pixel sprites · pets & furniture
Every cosmetic object is drawn on the same 10 × 10 cell sheet, so a sprite added at level 14 sits beside one drawn today without redrawing either.
FoxPET · FRONT VIEW · MIRRORED
Reading lampFURNITURE · FRONT · MIRRORED
Drawing rules
SHEET10 × 10 cells, hard edges only — no curves, gradients, or anti-aliasing.
PETSFront view, whole animal — head, chest, feet. Never a head floating alone.
OBJECTSSame front view, and everything is drawn as a 5-cell half then mirrored, so it reads level at any size.
COLOURFour per sprite: a body tone, its shadow, one light accent, one ink mark for the eye.
GROUNDRow 10 stays empty — the object rests on the baseline, never floats.
SCALEOne sheet, three renders: 2px cells in list headers, 3px in unlock cards, 5px in equip slots.
